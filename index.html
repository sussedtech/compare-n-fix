1. Introduction

Welcome to Compare n Fix ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data remains secure. 2. Core Application Data & "Offline-First" Architecture

Compare n Fix is engineered with a strict "offline-first" privacy model. Your data is your own.

  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, store, read, or monitor any of the list data, names, text, or CSV files you upload into the Service.
  • Local Processing: All data processing—including duplicate removal, formatting, and mathematical fuzzy matching—occurs strictly locally within the memory of your device's web browser.
  • No Servers: We do not maintain databases of user inputs, and your sensitive list data never touches our servers.

3. Local Storage

To improve your user experience, the Service utilizes your browser's local storage (localStorage) to auto-save your work in progress. This prevents data loss if you accidentally refresh the page. This data remains exclusively on your device. You can permanently clear this data at any time by clicking the "Reset All" button within the application or by clearing your browser's cache.

  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on your digital footprint.
  • Opting Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We believe in a frictionless, private experience. We do not use embedded tracking pixels, session recording software, or custom analytics scripts to monitor your keystrokes, clicks, or specific usage behaviors within the application.
